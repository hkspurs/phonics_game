# 🎮 P1 Adventure — Specialized AI Game Development Team

We have integrated 4 specialized agent roles from the `agency-agents` game development suite into the **P1 Adventure (升夢大冒險)** development pipeline:

---

## 👥 Meet the Team

### 1. 🎮 Game Designer (`game_designer`)
* **Vibe**: Thinks in loops, levers, and player motivations to architect compelling gameplay.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Core gameplay loops (Moment-to-moment quiz & runner, session station progression, long-term trophies & skins).
  * Economy balance (Coin earn rates, shop skin costs, star thresholds).
  * Age-appropriate difficulty progression tailored for Hong Kong Primary 1 (P.1 / 6-7 years old).
  * Onboarding flow and instant feedback reward systems.

### 2. 📖 Narrative Designer (`narrative_designer`)
* **Vibe**: Architects story systems where narrative and gameplay are inseparable.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Hong Kong cultural & curriculum world-building (Star Ferry, Space Museum, Ocean Park, Victoria Harbour, school & home life).
  * Mascot character voice pillars (Leo the Adventurer, friendly guide dialogues).
  * Educational storytelling that reinforces Chinese, Math, and English learning goals without overwhelming young children.
  * Environmental storytelling across the 10 station biomes.

### 3. 🎵 Game Audio Engineer (`game_audio_engineer`)
* **Vibe**: Makes every button tap, voice prompt, and musical fanfare feel alive and responsive.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Web Audio API & Phaser SoundManager pipeline.
  * Web SpeechSynthesis TTS integration (Cantonese `zh-HK` & English `en-US` normalization, delayed auto-read).
  * Audio feedback cues (Correct answer fanfare, card click, mistake wobble, runner coin pickup & jump SFX).
  * Mobile audio unlocking & zero-latency audio budget enforcement on iOS/Android.

### 4. 🎨 Technical Artist (`technical_artist`)
* **Vibe**: The bridge between artistic vision and engine reality.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Procedural vector rendering in Phaser 3 (Smooth geometry buttons, cards, banners, modal dialogues).
  * Particle VFX (Celebration confetti, floating clouds, sparks, chest burst effects).
  * Mobile responsive canvas scaling (`Scale.FIT` 1280x720 HD virtual resolution with zero coordinate drift).
  * 60 FPS mobile performance budgeting and Retina/High-DPI crisp visual output.

---

## 🚀 How to Collaborate
These subagents are actively registered in the Antigravity assistant system and can be invoked whenever you or the PM need specialized design reviews, audio adjustments, visual polish, or narrative expansions!
